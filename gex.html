thin days cluster — why yesterday's reading gates today
The rule: when the previous session's 10:30 ET 0DTE total GEX ranks in the bottom 20% of the trailing 120 days, M8BF skips the day — the fly is not posted and the day books as a no-trade. Applies to M8BF only; Straddle, GXBF, BOBF and the Tail hedge are unaffected. Histogram = the current 120-day window; the marker is the latest 10:30 reading, the shaded zone is skip territory.
