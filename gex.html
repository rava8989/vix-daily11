GEX Heatmap

Net dealer gamma per strike in $M — green = positive (dealers dampen moves there), red = negative (they amplify). SPX+SPY adds the two books: dollar-gamma is already in the same units (SPY's 1/10 notional cancels out in gamma×spot²), so only the strike axis is translated — SPY strikes × the live SPX/SPY ratio (~10.03, not 10 — dividends make the difference ≈5 SPX strikes), snapped to the 5-point grid. Both books hedge into the same futures, so the sum is the fuller picture of dealer pressure. The rule: when the previous session's 10:30 ET dealer-anchor reading ranks in the bottom 17% of the trailing 120 days, M8BF skips the day — the fly is not posted and the day books as a no-trade. Applies to M8BF only; Straddle, GXBF, BOBF and the Tail hedge are unaffected. Histogram = the current 120-day window; the marker is the latest reading, the shaded zone is unanchored territory.
