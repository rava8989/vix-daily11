The rule

1
Skip the trap days: CPI day, last trading day of the month, first trading day of the month. (Diagnostic: those days ran 60–61% WR at −$53 to −$98 avg — the first-of-month kept losing $600–770.)
2
Noon check: 0DTE book ≤ −10B → sell CALL spread, 10-wide, target ≈$4.00 credit (short strike auto-lands near the money). Sliding days don’t rally back.
3
1 PM check (if noon didn’t fire): book positive → sell PUT spread, 10-wide, target ≈$2.50 credit. Pinned days don’t dump.
4
Neither condition → no trade. Hold to settlement, never exit early. Risk per lot: ≈$600 (call side) / ≈$750 (put side).
